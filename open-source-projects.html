The thread across the projects

Designed for reality.

From a private job-search workflow to enterprise AI exploration, the same engineering constraints keep showing up.

01 / CONTROL

Authorization is explicit.

Agents can plan and explain. Consequential actions need clear policy boundaries and human-controlled approval where required.

02 / TRUST

Evidence is inspectable.

Grounded outputs, source citations, logs, test artifacts, and stated limitations are part of the user experience.

03 / RESILIENCE

Failure states matter.

Timeouts, denied actions, missing evidence, interrupted streams, and recovery paths deserve deliberate product design.

How to review the work: Open a project card, follow its demo or source, and expand its engineering decisions and limitations. For reusable patterns, see Frontend AI Patterns and Angular AI Copilot Starter. For an experimental QA prototype, see QA-Buddy.
