Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This is the portfolio of Ankit Parekh at ankit-parekh.org. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking scripts of mine. This page explains the two features that take input from you: the quick message form and the AI agent.

Quick message form

  • What you send: your name, email address, the topic you pick, an optional company name and your message.
  • What happens to it: it is emailed to me and used only to reply to you. It is not added to a mailing list and is not sold or shared.
  • What I keep: the email sits in my inbox until I delete it. The website's server does not store a copy.
  • Spam protection: your IP address is used briefly to limit how many messages can be sent in a minute. It is not stored.

AI agent and fit check

  • Your questions and any job description you paste are sent to the Anthropic API so it can write an answer from the profile on this site.
  • They are not stored by this website, and I do not keep a log of them. Anthropic handles API data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Please do not paste confidential or personal information about other people.
  • Answers are generated by an AI. Check anything important with me directly.

Hosting and fonts

  • The site and the agent run on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Like any host, they process standard technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver pages and keep them secure.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Your choices

To ask what I hold about you, or to have a message you sent deleted, email ankit77parekh@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
